3D RulioOrganisms

1D2D3D
Symmetric 3D cellular automata, evolved by single-point mutation. Each organism is a 3D body that changes at every step: grow it, scrub through its life, cut it open.

Rule space

A cell looks at itself and its six face neighbours. Rules treat all 48 rotations and reflections of the cube alike. Mutations to cases the organism never uses are neutral and cost nothing, so the budget scales with the case count.

Selection

Patterns that never die get fitness 0. A mutation is kept if fitness doesn't decrease.

Evolution

A mutant that never dies must be run all the way to the cutoff, and that cost grows with the fourth power of the cutoff. 100 is quick; 200 is about sixteen times slower.

Fitness

Gray = all lineages · dark line = selected lineage. About a third of lineages never get going; that is normal in 3D.

Genome

One square per rule case, grouped by center color; its color is the outcome. Dark-edged = used by this organism.
